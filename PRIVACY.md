# Sol Echo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Sol Echo does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** You don't need to sign in to play.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The game doesn't track you or use any analytics or advertising services.
- **No data leaves your device.** Your saved game is stored only on your device.
- **No third parties.** The game doesn't include third-party SDKs.

If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple shares that feedback (and any screenshots you choose to attach) with the developer. See Apple's TestFlight terms for details.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/belgarath534/rpg-game/issues.
