You wake in tall grass. No name. No food. No supplies.

The sun hangs pale and weak, as if someone dimmed it. Lake to the east. Mountains to the south. It doesn't matter now. You need to survive.

Sol Echo is a survival RPG set in a living fantasy world where the light is dying. Explore, gather, craft and fight your way from nothing to legend, and uncover why the sun is fading.

SURVIVE
• Keep your health, food, water and energy up as day turns to night
• Drink from the lake, forage berries and mushrooms, hunt and fish
• Cook your food over a fire, or risk eating it raw

EXPLORE AN OPEN WORLD
• Grasslands, whispering woods, a glassy lake, ash-grey mountains and haunted ruins
• An easy compass that always tells you what lies in every direction
• A map that fills in as you explore
• Hidden items that can mean the difference between life and death

CRAFT AND FIGHT
• Craft clubs, spears, axes, iron swords and armor from what you find
• Turn-based battles against wolves, bandits, trolls, the Drowned and the shades that hunt at night
• Level up and grow stronger

BECOME A SUN WIZARD
• Find ancient Sun Shrines for hints, healing and Echo Shards
• Gather three shards to awaken the light within you and cast Sunbolt
• Face the Hollow King and bring the light back to the world

MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Built from day one to work fully with VoiceOver
• Every stat, button and event is spoken clearly
• Supports Dynamic Type

Your journey saves automatically, so you can pick it up any time.
